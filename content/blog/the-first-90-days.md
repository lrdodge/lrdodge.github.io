---
title: Your first 90 days is about trust, not productivity
date: "2025-08-20"
---

The first 90 days at a new company are rough.

You're learning new systems, names, acronyms, team dynamics, priorities, unspoken rules—and trying not to break anything in the process.

It’s easy to fall into the trap of chasing impact early.
Prove your worth. Make a splash. Show them they made the right hire.

When you’re running instead of walking, it’s easy to step on toes, especially when you don’t yet know who’s in the room or how the room even works.

What helped me shift my perspective was realizing this:
Your job in the first 90 days isn’t to deliver. It’s to build trust.

Up. Down. Sideways. With your peers, your manager, your team, your cross-functional partners. Trust is the work.

And it’s not passive. Building trust means showing up, asking smart questions, getting curious, admitting when you don’t know something, and following through.

And the irony? When you focus on trust, you end up making a bigger impact anyway.
